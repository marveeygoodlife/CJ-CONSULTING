What We Do

Business Services You Can Rely On

From registering your business to keeping your records up to date, we help you take care of the important paperwork.

CAC Business Name Registration

Register your business name with CAC and get the documents you need to operate your business properly.

Private Limited Company (LTD) Registration

Set up your limited company properly and get the documents needed to run and grow your business.

Incorporated Trustees Registration

Register your NGO, church, club, association, or other non-profit group properly with the relevant authorities.

NGO, Church, Mosque & Club Registration

We help you complete the registration process and get the documents needed for your organisation.

School Registration

Get help with the registration process and the documents needed to set up your school properly.

Trademark Registration

Protect your business name, logo, slogan, or other brand details by registering your trademark.

Annual Returns Filing

Keep your CAC records up to date and avoid issues that can come from missing your annual returns.

CAC Lost Document Retrieval

Lost your CAC documents? We can help you retrieve the records and documents you need.

CAC Record Modifications & Updates

Update your CAC records when your business details change, such as your address, directors, or share capital.

Reactivation of Inactive CAC Status

If your business is no longer active on the CAC system, we can help you with the steps needed to restore it.

All Post-Incorporation Services

Need help after registering your business? We can help with updates, filings, and other ongoing business needs.

FIRS & JTB TIN Processing

Get help with your Tax Identification Number (TIN) for your business and other official needs.

TaxPro Max Registration & Updates

We help you with TaxPro Max registration, updates, tax filing, and other related services.

EFCC SCUML Certificate Processing

We help eligible businesses with their SCUML registration and certificate processing.

Audit Yearly Financial Statements

Get your yearly financial statements prepared and reviewed for your business, tax, or other official needs.

Contract Documents Application

Get help preparing and processing important contracts, applications, and other business documents.
